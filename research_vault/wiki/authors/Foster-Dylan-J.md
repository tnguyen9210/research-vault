# Dylan J. Foster

**Affiliation:** Microsoft Research
**Research areas:** Online learning, contextual bandits, RL theory, LM alignment

## Papers in this Vault

- [[Foster2025Foundation]] (2025) — computational-statistical tradeoffs for online LM alignment; SpannerSampling; proves coverage is necessary for efficient exploration

## Research Themes

Foster works at the intersection of statistical learning theory and online decision making. Recurring themes: oracle-efficient algorithms (DEC/DOEC framework), computational vs. statistical tradeoffs, and recently the theory of efficient RL/alignment with language models. Co-developed the Decision Estimation Coefficient (DEC) framework — see [[dec]].
