# Kwang-Sung Jun

**Affiliation:** University of Arizona  
**Research areas:** contextual bandits, betting-based confidence sequences, online and offline learning theory

## Papers in this Vault

- [[Ryu2025Improved]] (2025) — senior author; betting and freezing for improved offline contextual bandits

## Research Themes

Jun's work spans online and offline bandit learning, with a recurring focus on confidence bound construction via gambling/martingale techniques. Co-developed the Orabona-Jun (2024) universal portfolio approach to empirical-Bernstein-type bounds, which underlies the UP-LCB in [[Ryu2025Improved]].
