# Sergey Levine

**Affiliation:** Department of EECS, UC Berkeley
**Research areas:** Deep RL, offline RL, robotic learning, large-scale policy learning.

## Papers in this Vault

- [[Kostrikov2022Offline]] (2022) — senior author of IQL

## Research Themes

Senior author on a large fraction of the modern offline RL literature (CQL, AWAC, D4RL, IQL among others), with a consistent emphasis on methods that scale to real robotic data. The recurring thesis across that work — that distributional shift, not optimization, is the binding constraint in offline RL — is the premise [[Kostrikov2022Offline]] attacks by eliminating out-of-sample queries entirely.
