# Alex Rogers

**Affiliation:** University of Oxford (work in this vault done at the University of Southampton, ECS)
**Research areas:** Multi-agent systems, decentralized coordination, machine learning for sensor networks and energy/sustainability applications.

## Papers in this Vault

- [[TranThanh2010Epsilon]] (2010) — co-author on the paper introducing the [[budget-limited-mab]]

## Research Themes

Applies multi-agent and bandit methods to real-world resource-constrained systems — wireless sensor networks, smart-grid and energy management — where decision policies must respect hard physical budgets. The budget-limited MAB work grew out of energy-limited sensing applications.
