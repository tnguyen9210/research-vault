# Ming Yin

**Affiliation:** Department of Computer Science and Department of Statistics & Applied Probability, UC Santa Barbara (at time of publication)
**Research areas:** Statistical theory of offline RL; instance-dependent and variance-aware sample complexity; RL with function approximation.

## Papers in this Vault

- [[Yin2023Offline]] (2023) — PFQL/VAFQL: first instance-dependent offline RL bound under nonlinear ([[differentiable-function-approximation]]) function approximation, with a lower bound matching to $\sqrt{d}$

## Research Themes

A sustained program pushing offline RL guarantees from worst-case toward [[instance-dependent-bounds]], moving up the model hierarchy one class at a time — tabular (VPVI, Yin & Wang 2021), then variance-aware linear (Yin et al. 2022), then differentiable/nonlinear ([[Yin2023Offline]]). Variance awareness recurs as the mechanism for shaving horizon factors and exposing per-instance structure.
