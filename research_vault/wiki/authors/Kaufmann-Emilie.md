# Emilie Kaufmann

**Affiliation:** Univ. Lille, Inria, CNRS, Centrale Lille (UMR 9198-CRIStAL)  
**Research areas:** Bandits, pure exploration, best arm identification, MCTS

## Papers in this Vault
- [[Dam2024Power]] (2024) — co-author on Stochastic-Power-UCT; MCTS convergence in stochastic MDPs

## Research Themes
Theoretical foundations of bandit algorithms and pure exploration. Co-developer of the TAS algorithm (Track-And-Stop, Kaufmann & Garivier 2016), the asymptotically optimal $\delta$-PAC BAI algorithm that directly precedes [[Kanarios2024Cost]]'s CTAS. Her work spans BAI ([[best-arm-identification]]), MCTS ([[monte-carlo-tree-search]]), and Bayesian bandits.
