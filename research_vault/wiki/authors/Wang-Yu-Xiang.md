# Yu-Xiang Wang

**Affiliation:** Department of Computer Science, UC Santa Barbara (at time of publication)
**Research areas:** Statistical learning theory; offline RL and off-policy evaluation; differential privacy; adaptive data analysis.

## Papers in this Vault

- [[Yin2023Offline]] (2023) — senior author; PFQL/VAFQL and the instance-dependent analysis over differentiable function classes

## Research Themes

Senior author on the UCSB line of offline RL theory that has progressively replaced worst-case concentrability arguments with [[instance-dependent-bounds]] — tabular, then linear with variance awareness, then nonlinear. The consistent methodological commitment is to guarantees that are adaptive to the data at hand and paired with lower bounds.
