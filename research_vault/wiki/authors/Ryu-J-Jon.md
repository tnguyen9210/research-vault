# J. Jon Ryu

**Affiliation:** Massachusetts Institute of Technology  
**Research areas:** offline policy optimization, betting-based confidence bounds, information theory

## Papers in this Vault

- [[Ryu2025Improved]] (2025) — introduces PUB and UP-LCB for variance-adaptive off-policy selection with unbounded importance weights

## Research Themes

Ryu's work applies gambling and martingale theory (Cover's universal portfolio, betting-based confidence sequences) to statistical estimation problems. The core theme is constructing tight, parameter-free confidence bounds that adapt to the underlying distribution without requiring prior knowledge of its moments.
